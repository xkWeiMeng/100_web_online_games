Conway's Game of Life

A cellular automaton devised by mathematician John Conway

  • Click / drag to toggle cells
  • Right-click drag to pan the grid
  • Scroll to zoom in/out
  • Space to play/pause
  • N to step one generation
  • C to clear the grid

Rules: A live cell with 2-3 neighbors survives. A dead cell with exactly 3 neighbors becomes alive. All other cells die or stay dead.
